About Me

I like building things that look good and actually work.

I'm Anisur Rahman Noyon Sarker from Dhaka, Bangladesh. For more than five years I've spent most of my days cutting footage, designing motion, and crafting the small visual details that make a video feel intentional. What started with KineMaster on a phone gradually became Filmora, then Adobe Premiere Pro — and the same curiosity that made me chase better edits also pulled me into code.

On the technical side I write Python, build responsive interfaces, and explore how blockchain can lock evidence so it can't be quietly altered. My most meaningful technical work so far is a Blockchain Forensic Image Preservation Framework — a system that combines deep-learning classification with smart contracts to keep forensic images verifiable.

I don't see creative work and engineering as separate careers. The same instinct that tells me when a cut is too long also tells me when a function is doing too much. That overlap is where I prefer to live.
